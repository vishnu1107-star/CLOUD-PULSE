### SECTION 3 – Project Information

* **Project Title:**  
  `CloudPulse: Autonomous Multi-Cloud Cost Reclamation & Instant Hydration Engine`

* **Problem Domain:**  
  `AI Infrastructure / FinOps / Green Tech & Business Automation`

* **13. SDG Alignment (Select 3):**  
  - `SDG 9 – Industry, Innovation and Infrastructure`  
  - `SDG 12 – Responsible Consumption and Production`  
  - `SDG 13 – Climate Action`  

* **14. Problem Statement (Max 200 words):**  
  > Over $17 Billion is wasted globally every year due to non-production cloud resources (staging, development, QA) running 24/7 during off-hours, nights, and weekends. Existing FinOps platforms operate purely in an advisory capacity, producing static PDF reports and ticketing digests. Software engineering teams frequently ignore these recommendations due to fears of breaking service dependencies or incurring high developer re-hydration friction. Furthermore, legacy scripts rely on coarse, CPU-only checks that prematurely pause databases during active background jobs or long-running debugging sessions, causing unintended service downtime.

* **15. Proposed Solution (Max 250 words):**  
  > CloudPulse is an autonomous, multi-cloud infrastructure lifecycle engine that bridges FinOps advisory with zero-downtime automated execution. It uses multi-signal telemetry fusion (evaluating CPU, network bandwidth, and active DB/HTTP sockets simultaneously over rolling 30-minute windows) alongside tag-aware governance to identify non-production workloads safely. Upon detection, CloudPulse automatically pauses idle virtual machines (AWS EC2, GCP Compute) and scales Kubernetes deployments to 0 replicas while executing ghost resource sweeping (purging unattached storage disks and orphan static Elastic IPs). It includes a sub-3-second instant warm hydration protocol (via Web UI and Slack ChatOps) for zero-friction developer re-activation.

* **16. How is Artificial Intelligence used in your solution? (Max 250 words):**  
  > CloudPulse uses predictive multi-signal telemetry analytics and pattern-learning algorithms to evaluate resource utilization across rolling time-series metric streams. Instead of simple static thresholds, the engine fuses CPU utilization (<2.0%), network throughput (<10 KB/s), and active connection socket counts (DB & HTTP == 0) to distinguish genuine idle states from active developer sessions or background jobs. Furthermore, CloudPulse leverages historical telemetry logs to model off-hours developer activity patterns, predicting when staging environments should be pre-hydrated to optimize developer velocity and avoid cold-start delays.

* **17. What makes your solution unique? (Max 200 words):**  
  > CloudPulse stands out due to 4 core competitive advantages: (1) **Zero-Outage Multi-Signal Evaluation:** Eliminates false positives by combining CPU, network, and DB socket metrics. (2) **Sub-3-Second Hydration Protocol:** Restores paused environments instantly via 1-click Web UI or Slack ChatOps (`/cloudpulse wakeup`), replacing 30-60 minute manual CloudOps bottlenecks. (3) **Autonomous Ghost Resource Reaper:** Continuously sweeps and purges unattached storage volumes and orphan static IPs with automated 30-day snapshot rollbacks. (4) **Auditable Carbon Offset Engine:** Computes verifiable carbon emission reductions (kg CO2 avoided) for enterprise ESG compliance.

* **18. Who are the intended beneficiaries/users?**  
  > 1. Enterprise DevOps & CloudOps Teams  
  > 2. FinOps & Infrastructure Managers  
  > 3. Software Engineering Organizations (Developers, QA Teams)  
  > 4. Corporate ESG & Sustainability Officers  

* **19. Expected Impact:**  
  * **Economic Impact:** Achieves up to 45% reclamation of non-production cloud infrastructure spending ($17B global problem) with zero operational overhead.  
  * **Social Impact:** Eliminates developer friction and alert fatigue; sub-3-second re-activation preserves developer velocity and engineering productivity.  
  * **Environmental Impact:** Direct, measurable carbon footprint reduction by calculating exact kilowatt-hours saved and translating them into verifiable carbon emission offsets (kg CO2 saved based on grid emission factors).
